Libraries next to the program

Their index lives in shoebox-windows\libraries\. The folders themselves are only read.

Delete the index of “Fotos”?

The photos on the server are not touched. What shoebox knows about them is deleted from this computer: the list of photos, previews, and everything you added in shoebox, such as tags, dates, places, people and pets you named.

If the program folder cannot be written to

“Next to the program” is not available: shoebox cannot write to C:\Program Files\shoebox-windows. Move the shoebox folder to your Documents or Desktop, or keep the index on the drive.
