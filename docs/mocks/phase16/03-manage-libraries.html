Libraries kept next to the program / not on drive Not on drive: the .shoebox folder is not created inside the drive’s root. Instead, a libraries folder is added next to the program, and the index of each library is kept there. Nothing is written into your photo folder.

Their index lives in shoebox-windows\libraries\. The folders themselves are only read.

Delete the index of “Fotos”?

The photos on the server are not touched. What shoebox knows about them is deleted from this computer: the list of photos, previews, and everything you added in shoebox, such as tags, dates, places, people and pets you named.

If the program folder cannot be written to

“Not on the drive” is not available: shoebox cannot write to C:\Program Files\shoebox-windows. Move the shoebox folder to your Documents or Desktop, or keep the index on the drive.
