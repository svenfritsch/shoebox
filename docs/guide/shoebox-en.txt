YOUR PHOTO LIBRARY, ON THE DRIVE.
=================================

shoebox is a small program that lives on your external drive next to your
photos. It indexes them, shows them in a fast timeline in your browser, and
helps you tag, sort, import and de-duplicate, without ever copying them or
touching their metadata.

- Originals stay untouched
- Lives on your drive
- Private: no cloud, no account
- Browse from the iPad too
- macOS today · Linux & Windows next

[Screenshot: The timeline: months, folders, search, type filter and year
jump.]

What shoebox can do
-------------------

One page in your browser, a timeline of everything on the drive.

- Timeline
   All photos and videos by month, with a year jump, folder tree and a viewer
   with full-size info.

- Search & filter
   Type a person, pet, tag or folder. Combine keywords for a more complex
   search. Filter by photos, videos or Live Photos, or show only your
   favorites ♥.

- Tags
   Your own tags on many photos at once. Folders count as tags too. Stored in
   the index, never in the photos.

- Move & organise
   Move photos to a folder (created if needed). RAW, Live Photo and sidecar
   files travel along.

- Import
   Pick the photos of an event or drop them into the window: they land in a
   new event folder and keep their dates.

- Duplicates
   Finds identical, resized, edited and look-alike photos. You review; the
   best copy always stays.

- Safe trash
   Deleted photos go to a trash on the drive. Put them back, or delete them
   for good.

- People & pets
   Optional recognition groups faces, cats and dogs. You name them; shoebox
   suggests the rest.

- Several drives
   Browse the photos of several drives in one common timeline, even when they
   sit on different disks. Backups are recognised and checked.

The screen at a glance
----------------------

Everything is on one page. Here is where things are.

1. Folders menu
   ☰ shows or hides the sidebar on narrow screens.

2. Search box
   People, pets, tags and folders; pick several to combine them.

3. Type
   Show only photos, videos or Live Photos.

4. Favorites ♥
   Show only the photos you have hearted. Also found by typing favorite in the
   search box.

5. Year
   Jump straight to a year.

6. Select ☑
   Pick photos to move, tag or trash.

7. Import ⤒
   Add photos and videos from this computer or the iPad.

8. Quick links
   All photos · Duplicates · Trash · Settings.

9. Folders
   Your folders, with counts. Tags, Faces and Drives appear here when you have
   them.

10. Timeline
   Months with counts. Click a photo to open the viewer.

11. Language & status
   Switch English / German; see scan, thumbnail and drive status.

A typical session
Plug in the drive and double-click Start shoebox. Scan (quick when little has
changed) and open the photo app. Import the photos of an event: pick them,
name the event, and shoebox creates the event folder. Then tag and sort. Look
at Duplicates now and then, and run the Backup check after every backup.

Get started in three steps
--------------------------

macOS, Intel (10.13 or newer) and Apple Silicon. Nothing to install.

1. Put it on the drive
   Unpack shoebox-macos.tar.gz from the release. Copy shoebox-macos and Start
   shoebox.command to the top folder of your photo drive (next to your photo
   folders). Optional: the recognizer folder for faces. The archive also holds
   this guide in the folder guide.

2. Start the launcher
   Double-click Start shoebox.command. A Terminal window opens (leave it open)
   and the launcher appears in your browser. If macOS asks, right-click the
   file and choose Open.

3. Scan, then open
   Add your drive (click it, or type /Volumes/MyDrive), press Scan, then Start
   photo app. The first scan reads every file once and takes a while on a big
   drive; later scans only look at what changed.

Launcher tasks
Scan: find new, moved and changed photos
Verify: check that nothing was lost or damaged
Recognize: find faces (and, with Recognize pets, cats and dogs)
Backup check: is the 2nd folder a complete copy of the 1st?

[Screenshot: The launcher: 1 choose the drive or folder, 2 pick a task, 3 open

Good to know
- Videos get preview images if you put a static ffmpeg next to the program
  (.shoebox/bin/ffmpeg). Without it, the browser grabs a frame instead.
- shoebox keeps its index and thumbnails in a hidden .shoebox folder on the
  drive, so the drive can move to another computer.
- Closing the Terminal window stops shoebox.

The app speaks English and German: pick the language at the bottom of the
sidebar.

Browse and view
---------------

Scroll through the timeline, jump by year, open a photo.

Timeline
Photos are grouped by capture date, newest first. The Year menu jumps; the
month heading shows where you are. A photo without a capture date takes the
month in the name of its event folder (2020-07, 2020.07, 20-07 or 20.07), or
else the earlier of the file’s created and modified dates; the info panel then
marks the date as estimated.

Folders
The sidebar mirrors the folders on your drive. Click one to see only its
photos. Rename or move a folder from its chip.

Viewer
Click a photo. ⓘ opens the info panel: date (marked estimated when it is not
the day the photo was taken), camera, size, tags, people, and buttons to open
the folder or copy the path.

Favorites
The ♥ in the top bar of the viewer, or the key f, marks the photo as a
favorite. In the timeline the same heart sits in the top right corner of each
photo (it appears when you point at a photo; hearted photos always show it).
Click it again to take it off. Under the hood a favorite is just your own tag
favorite, kept in the index like any tag, never in the photo.

Rotate
r turns left, Shift+R right. For JPEGs this changes only the two bytes of the
rotation flag; for other formats the turn exists in shoebox only.

[Screenshot: The viewer with its info panel.]

Keyboard in the viewer
← →: previous / next
i: show / hide the info panel
Space: open / close the viewer (on a photo picked with the arrow keys)
f: add to / remove from favorites
r · Shift+R: rotate left / right
Esc: close the viewer

Find and organise
-----------------

Search, select, tag and move: all of it works on many photos at once.

Search
Type in the search box: suggestions appear for tags, people and folders. Pick
several and they become chips that narrow the result; × removes one, Clear all
starts over. To find your favorites press the ♥ button next to Type, or type
favorite (English) or favorit (German) and pick ♥ Favorites; it combines with
everything else, e.g. favorites of one person or one year.

Select
Press ☑, then click photos (Shift-click for a range; on the iPad just tap).
“Select all” on a month heading takes the whole month.

Tag
Add tag… puts your own tag on the selection. Tags live in shoebox’s index,
never in the photo files.

Move
Move… asks for a folder. New folders are created, nothing is ever overwritten,
and RAW, Live Photo and sidecar files move along. The photos’ own tags always
go with them. “Keep folder tags” also keeps the old folder’s tags as own tags;
folder tags always follow the new folder.

Import, duplicates and trash
----------------------------

Import
Press ⤒ (or drop files on the window). Choose year, month and an event name:
shoebox creates the event folder YYYY-MM Event, the files keep their dates,
and files already in the library are skipped. In Settings you choose how new
event folders are named: 4 or 2 digits for the year, a hyphen or a dot between
year and month, and a space, _, ., - or nothing before the name, for example
YY.MM_Event. Folders you already have are not renamed, and all these forms are
recognised.

Duplicates
Duplicates lists four kinds: identical, same photo in another size, original
and edited, and look-alikes. Tick delete this copy on what you want gone; the
best file stays and at least one per group always does. Tags and capture dates
of a removed copy carry over.

Shortcuts
Clear Same Folder Copies and Clear Lower Quality Copies clean up the clear-cut
cases in one go. Groups of similar photos need your decision: choose Different
photos or Versions of one photo, and shoebox remembers it so the group does
not return.

Trash
Nothing is deleted right away. Trashed photos wait in .shoebox/trash on the
drive: Put back or Delete for good.

People and pets
---------------

Optional: shoebox finds faces, cats and dogs; you give them names.

Find them
Install the recognizer once (recognizer/install.sh /Volumes/MyDrive). Then
close the photo app and run Recognize (and Recognize pets) in the launcher. A
Faces section appears in the sidebar.

Name them
Under Unnamed, shoebox shows cards of similar faces. Type a name once and the
whole card is named; later, confirm (✓) or reject (✗) the suggestions. Group
people, merge two cards of one person, mark strangers with Ignore.

In the viewer
The info panel lists who is on the photo and draws a box around each face or
pet. Click a name to see all their photos, or draw a box around a face or pet
that was missed.

Search
Names work in the search box like tags, and so do kinds such as “all cats”.
Combine a person with a tag, a folder or another person.

New photos
Recognition does not start by itself after an import yet. Close the photo app
and run Recognize again: it only looks at the new photos.

Drives, backups and the iPad
----------------------------

Several drives
Start shoebox with more than one drive and each one appears in the sidebar.
All drives gives you one common timeline across all of them, and shows
backups, photos that exist on more than one drive, and people across drives. A
drive that is unplugged is shown as offline.

Backup check
Choose the original first and the backup second. shoebox compares the two
indexes (no photo is read) and lists what is not on the backup yet, what
differs and what exists only on the backup. Also re-read the backup finds bit
rot.

iPad & other devices
In Terminal: shoebox serve /Volumes/MyDrive --lan. It prints an address and a
PIN; open the address in Safari on the same Wi-Fi and enter the PIN once.
Swipe in the viewer, tap to select.

Command line (optional)
shoebox scan <folder>: index new, moved and changed files
shoebox verify <folder>: re-read files and compare with the index
shoebox recognize <folder> [--pets]: find faces (and pets)
shoebox backup <original> <backup>: is the backup complete?
shoebox serve <folder> [--lan] [--pin …]: open the photo app

Your photos are safe
--------------------

The design rule is simple: shoebox reads your originals and never writes
anything into them.

- Read-only
   Metadata, thumbnails and tags are kept in the index on the drive, never
   inside a photo. Capture dates and created dates never change.

- Guarded
   Every scan is checked: size, dates and a full hash before and after. Any
   difference is an error.

- Only on your command
   Originals change only through your actions: move, rename, trash, import.
   Always a rename, never a copy, and only if the file still matches the
   index.

- Local
   No cloud, no account. By default only this computer can connect; other
   devices need --lan and a PIN.

If something looks off
----------------------

The grid is empty. Run Scan in the launcher first, then start the photo app.

New photos are missing. Scan again. It is quick: only new and changed files
are read.

A video has no preview. Add ffmpeg (see “Good to know”) and restart the photo
app.

The iPad cannot connect. Same Wi-Fi? Started with --lan? Enter the PIN printed
in Terminal. Restarting shoebox asks for the PIN again.

A drive shows as offline. Plug it in again; shoebox picks it up when you
reload.
